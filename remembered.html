Profile

Trained on the school field, now in the engineering faculty.

I am Lasen Wikramawardena, an electrical and electronic engineering undergraduate in the Faculty of Engineering at the University of Sri Jayewardenepura. I live in Ratmalana. The degree is a four-year Bachelor of the Science of Engineering Honours.

I began at Isipathana College, Colombo, in Grade 1 and stayed through Grade 11, where I played table tennis and cricket. For Grade 12 and the G.C.E. Advanced Level, I moved to Ananda College, Colombo, and completed my A/Ls there.

The engineering work now is circuits, signals, digital electronics, and power and telecommunication systems. I am interested in designs that survive both the calculation and the test bench.

Engineering

The scope of the electrical and electronic engineering degree, from the component to the system.

  1. 01

    Circuit fundamentals

    Analysis, measurement, and the habit of checking a result against both the equation and the instrument.

  2. 02

    Analog & digital electronics

    From discrete circuits to digital systems, including the point where a design has to live on real hardware.

  3. 03

    Signals & processing

    How information sits inside a waveform, and what it takes to recover, shape, or pass it on.

  4. 04

    Power & telecommunication systems

    Machines, power networks, and communication systems at a scale bigger than a single board.
